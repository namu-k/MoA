# Modes

- `shape`: testable problem, assumptions, non-goals, and evidence gaps.
- `design`: UX, API, data, and implementation contract boundaries.
- `decide`: competing options, trade-offs, reversibility, and evidence.
- `review`: conformance to a governing plan/spec/tests and regression risk.

Choose only these presets. Use MoA when multiple defensible options or costly disagreement exist; do not use it for deterministic formatting or a fact settled by one test.
